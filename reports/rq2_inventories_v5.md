# RQ2: RQ1's contrasts under seven token inventories, logistic v5 (DEC-22, DEC-23)

Generated by `rq2_compare.py` (`data/results/rq2_compare_v5/summary.json`) from the committed logistic v5 runs (DEC-23), which choose on the validation day among v4's fits of every sub-condition; inventory A is RQ1's own run, also under v5, so like is compared with like. The v4 comparison stays as the record (`reports/rq2_inventories_v4.md`). All inventories label the same 1,547 comparison targets, and targets, folds, S, the UNK policy, the model and its selection rule are RQ1's (PLAN §7). Raw log-likelihoods are not compared across inventories (PLAN `[R2-3]`): every number below is a difference within one inventory, a ratio of such differences, or a difference of those ratios. Each inventory's lattice is in its own report.

Day-bootstrap CIs: 10,000 replicates (seed 42); each replicate's resampled days are shared by every inventory, so differences against A are paired. The draws reproduce each run's recorded contrast CIs. With 14 days the CIs are approximate, and limited data and capacity push contrasts toward zero: a CI that spans zero may mean too little data (PLAN §9).
